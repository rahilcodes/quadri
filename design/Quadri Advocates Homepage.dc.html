About the advocate

Every matter is someone's life. We treat it that way.

Mohd. Moin Ahmed Quadri enrolled at the Bar in 1997 and has practised from Narayanguda since. He appears before the High Court for the State of Telangana, the Hyderabad City Civil Court and the consumer fora.

The chambers take civil, criminal, family, consumer and commercial matters, and provide notarial services. Every client meets Mr. Quadri himself.

Chambers sit in the evening, after court hours, so that clients can attend after their own working day.
